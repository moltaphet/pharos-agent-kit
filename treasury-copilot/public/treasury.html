How a payment moves

Every step is shown on the page, with its source.

  1. 1

    Read

    The agent asks the Pharos Developer Docs MCP for the network facts and reads balance, allowance and the invoice registry with the SkillEngine.

  2. 2

    Check

    It inspects the recipient, applies your cap and allowlist, and simulates the exact call on Atlantic.

  3. 3

    Sign

    You approve in your wallet. The approval covers this invoice amount only, never unlimited.

  4. 4

    Verify

    The receipt must match payer, router, calldata, amount and the on-chain event before it counts as paid.

Safety model

What the agent, the page and the contract can and cannot do.

No keys, anywhere

The agent, the API and this page never hold or ask for a private key. The only signer is your own wallet.

You sign every move

Funds move only through a transaction you approve in your wallet. Approvals cover the exact invoice amount.

Limits come first

A per-invoice cap and an optional recipient allowlist are checked before anything is prepared.

It checks who gets paid

The agent inspects the recipient on-chain. A smart contract or a never-used address makes it ask you to confirm before signing unlocks.

It stops instead of guessing

If the docs, the chain reads or the simulation fail, it stops and says why. Unknown outcomes are saved and never retried.

One reference, one payment

The router refuses a repeated reference for the same payer and holds no balance. Receipts are matched to the exact on-chain event.

tUSDC is a demonstration token with no monetary value, and this is a testnet product, not financial advice. Threat model ↗ · Architecture ↗
