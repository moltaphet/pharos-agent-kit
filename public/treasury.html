04Safety model

No keys, anywhere

The agent, the API and this page never hold or ask for a private key. The only signer is your own wallet.

You sign every move

Funds move only through a transaction you approve in your wallet. Approvals are for the exact invoice amount, never unlimited.

Limits before approval

A per-invoice cap and an optional recipient allowlist are checked first. A failed check prepares nothing to sign.

Stopped, not guessed

If documentation, chain reads or simulation fail, the agent stops with a clear reason. Unknown outcomes are saved and never retried automatically.

One reference, one payment

The router refuses a repeated invoice reference for the same payer, and holds no balance of its own.

Verified from the chain

A payment counts only when the receipt matches payer, router, calldata, amount and the exact on-chain event.
